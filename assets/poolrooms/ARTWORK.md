# Poolrooms — Beyond the Bend

The source artwork is the approved, generated Daycraft concept **Aquamarine Poolrooms Beyond the Bend**. The local WebP is a size-optimised encoding of that image (1672 × 941). It supplies the opening camera's projected detail, scene swatch and entry still.

The rooms, bridge, steps, flume, manta, falling sheet and water surface are original runtime meshes. The projection fades at the first camera's frustum edges and is disabled at the two other locations. The light atlas is baked from the shipped geometry, with no lights or windows open to an outdoor sky. The two other stills are rendered from those actual camera locations with the runtime shaders. No remote assets or generation services are used by the app.
