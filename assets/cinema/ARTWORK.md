# Cinema exhibit artwork

The project asset is `assets/cinema/exhibit-atlas.png`. It was created with the built-in image-generation tool, using the user's supplied McDonald’s Movie Memorabilia Corridor image as the appearance reference. No supplied image was overwritten. The atlas provides the six illustrative inserts inside the 3D cases. It is not a set of scanned or independently verified film props.

## Generation prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: a SINGLE photographic texture atlas for display cabinet BACKING ART in a 3D cinema memorabilia cafe. Image 1 is the primary appearance reference, NOT an edit target. Create a precise equal 3-column by 2-row grid, six rectangular panels, edge to edge with no margins or gutters, each panel a straight-on close photograph of a warm museum exhibit in a dark vitrine interior. No frame edges, no glass reflections, no text or labels anywhere, no people. Top-left panel: the Jurassic Park raptor animatronic head on a slender neck, beige olive scaly wrinkled reptile skin, open jaw with fine sharp teeth, tall white speckled dinosaur egg beside it, glossy lush leaves around base, dark green-black backing. Top-center panel: a charming polished ivory and chrome Earth 2 style museum robot, black spherical mirrored helmet visor, articulated arms, rounded torso and joints, against deep navy starfield background, as in the reference. Top-right panel: Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles 1990 animatronic Raphael head on a short dark stand, distinctive RED fabric eye bandana, green textured foam-latex skin, eye openings with brown eyes, smiling expressive mouth, neck and upper shoulders only, three-quarter facing left, black backing. Bottom-left panel: Indiana Jones worn brown fedora above a looped brown leather bullwhip hanging vertically, small brass cup below, sepia production sketch background. Bottom-center panel: Who Framed Roger Rabbit exhibit with upright white four-finger cartoon glove, red fedora, small wooden ACME-style box but no text, warm cream sketch background. Bottom-right panel: detailed stainless steel Back to the Future DeLorean time machine scale model seen front three-quarter, accurate angled windshield, black rear time travel components, pipes, brushed silver panels, black pedestal and navy-black background. Lighting: warm soft cabinet spotlights from above, museum photography, realistic detailed physical miniature/latex/felt/chrome materials. Each panel entirely contains its exhibit, with 8% breathing room so nothing crosses panel boundaries. Rectangular landscape final texture atlas, 3:2 overall aspect, equal square cells. Render only the atlas, not a room, not a concept sheet with headings. Preserve the reference prop identity and appearance as closely as possible.
